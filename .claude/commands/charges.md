---
description: What a contract has earned at its own schedule of rates between two dates, split into signed off and not signed off.
---

1. Run `npm run drill -- charges <contract> [--from=date] [--to=date]`.
2. Present both tables. The signed-off total is what can be claimed; the other is what is waiting on the client.
3. If a line looks wrong, open the plods behind it with `/plods --contract=..`.
