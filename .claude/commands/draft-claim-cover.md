---
description: Draft the cover email that goes with a progress claim: the period, metres by method, the amount, client standby named, and the plods held back.
---

1. Run `npm run drill -- claim <contract> --period-end=<date> --json` (or read the saved claim) and `npm run drill -- utilisation --days=31 --json`.
2. Write to `drafts/claim-cover-<claim>.md`: period, holes and metres by method, the amount excluding GST, any client standby with its reason, and any plods held back for sign-off with what they are worth.
3. Never send it.
